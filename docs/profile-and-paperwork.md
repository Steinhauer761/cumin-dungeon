# Profiles & Platform Paperwork

## Individual profiles

Every member and creator gets an individual account/profile. Keep public profile data separate from private account, verification, billing, and moderation data.

### Member profile
- Display name / handle.
- Avatar and optional profile artwork.
- Short bio.
- Pronouns and optional identity fields where appropriate.
- Interests / room preferences.
- Favorite creators / rooms.
- Privacy and discoverability settings.
- Private-chat request preferences.
- Approved-media preferences.
- Blocked users.
- Notification settings.
- Profile visibility: public, members-only, or private.

### Creator profile
- Stage/display name.
- Avatar, banner, and approved artwork.
- Bio and categories.
- Availability / schedule.
- Rooms hosted.
- Games assigned.
- Gift catalog / featured gifts.
- Goals and polls.
- Private-request rules.
- Membership/booking options where applicable.
- Creator analytics and earnings are private to the creator/admin.

### Data separation
Never expose or use public profile fields as a substitute for identity verification. Legal name, government-ID information, verification results, payment information, safety cases, and internal moderation notes belong in protected systems with role-based access.

## Required platform paperwork

Create these as versioned documents before public launch. These are product/legal requirements to be reviewed by qualified Canadian counsel, not legal advice.

1. Terms of Service / Membership Agreement.
2. Privacy Policy.
3. Community Rules / Acceptable Use Policy.
4. Creator Agreement.
5. Creator Content & Rights / License Agreement.
6. Age Assurance & Verification Notice.
7. Consent and Private Interaction Rules.
8. Messaging & Media Policy.
9. Recording / Streaming Rules.
10. Gifts, Tips, Purchases & Refund Policy.
11. Creator Payout Terms.
12. Content Moderation and Enforcement Policy.
13. Reporting / Appeals Policy.
14. Copyright / Takedown Policy.
15. Safety and prohibited-content policy.
16. Cookie / analytics disclosure where applicable.

20. Member onboarding acknowledgement.

## Onboarding acknowledgements

Users should actively acknowledge the rules relevant to their account and receive a record of the policy version accepted, timestamp, and consent/acknowledgement event.

Creator onboarding should separately record acceptance of creator-specific terms, content rights, payout rules, moderation rules, and platform safety requirements.

## Privacy-by-design requirements

- Collect only information needed for a defined purpose.
- Keep identity/age-assurance data separate from ordinary profile data.
- Prefer receiving an age-qualified result rather than retaining raw identity documents where the selected provider supports this.
- Publish retention periods and deletion rules.
- Provide appropriate access, correction, deletion, and appeal workflows.
- Use role-based access for identity, billing, safety, and moderation data.
- Maintain an audit trail for sensitive administrative actions.
- Document processors/vendors and data-sharing purposes.

Canada's privacy regulator issued updated age-assurance guidance in May 2026 emphasizing proportional collection, privacy-protective design, limited use of age-assurance results, and appeal mechanisms. The final implementation should be checked against applicable Canadian and provincial law before launch.
