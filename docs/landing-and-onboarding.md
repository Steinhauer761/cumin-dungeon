# Landing & Onboarding

This document turns the current prototype gate into a production-oriented first journey.

## Landing page

The landing page should feel like the entrance to a private castle, not a generic adult-site template.

Primary structure:

1. Hero / Grand Entrance
   - Cum IN Dungeon wordmark using the refined red-script treatment.
   - Dragon crest and dark wood / red velvet / antique-gold visual language.
   - Clear 18+ positioning.
   - Primary CTA: Enter the Dungeon.
   - Secondary CTA: Become a Creator.

2. What is Cum IN Dungeon?
   - Private digital venue concept.
   - Live rooms, social rooms, creator experiences, games, gifts and scheduled events.
   - Consent and privacy are core product principles.

3. Explore the venue
   - Grand Hall.
   - Category rooms.
   - Halo Lounge.
   - Halo Theater.
   - VIP / After Hours.
   - Creator rooms.

4. How it works
   - Verify age.
   - Create an account.
   - Choose interests / room categories.
   - Enter rooms.
   - Request private chats only with recipient approval.
   - Support creators through gifts, goals and memberships.

5. Creator section
   - Apply / verify.
   - Build a creator profile.
   - Choose room categories.
   - Configure games and room mechanics.
   - Create goals and gifts.
   - Track earnings and engagement.

6. Trust & safety
   - 18+ only.
   - Clear prohibited-content rules.
   - Reporting and blocking.
   - Automated safety screening.
   - Human review / escalation where required.
   - Privacy-first retention and safety holds.

7. Footer
   - Terms.
   - Privacy.
   - Venue Rules.
   - Safety / Report.
   - Creator application.
   - Support.

## Member onboarding

After the landing CTA:

1. Confirm 18+ eligibility and jurisdiction availability.
2. Accept Terms, Privacy Policy and Venue Rules.
3. Create account / authenticate.
4. Complete required age verification through the selected provider.
5. Choose display name and profile basics.
6. Choose interests and preferred room categories.
7. Configure notification / privacy preferences.
8. Show a short consent-and-chat tutorial:
   - Public room chat is public to that room.
   - Private chat requires acceptance.
   - Media requests require acceptance.
   - Sender chooses whether an attachment is downloadable.
   - Block/report controls are always available.
9. Enter the Grand Hall.

## Creator onboarding

Separate creator path from member onboarding.

1. Start creator application.
2. Confirm age / identity through a vetted provider.
3. Accept creator agreement, content rules and payout terms.
4. Complete profile and stage name.
5. Select categories and room types.
6. Configure starter games.
7. Configure gifts / goals.
8. Configure private-request rules.
9. Complete moderation / safety orientation.
10. Submit for approval.
11. On approval, publish creator page and room schedule.

## Product principles

- No forced public exposure of private conversations.
- Private messaging is opt-in for both participants.
- Attachments are ephemeral by default and follow the 14-day retention policy unless a legitimate safety/legal hold applies.
- Download permission is chosen by the sender per attachment.
- Creator controls remain central to room mechanics.
- Monetization features must never bypass consent controls.
- Production age verification, payments, streaming, moderation and legal requirements must be implemented before launch.
