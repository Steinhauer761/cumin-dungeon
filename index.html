Club-wide standards

Venue Rules

Adults only

Every member, creator and depicted person must be 18 or older and able to provide valid verification.

Consent is mandatory

No coercion, threats, harassment, unwanted contact, exploitation or content involving anyone who has not clearly agreed to participate.

No recording or redistribution

Do not capture, download, repost or share another person’s room, image, message or personal information without explicit permission.

Respect room controls

Creators and moderators may remove participants, limit chat, end sessions and report accounts. Blocking, reporting and leaving must remain available.

Zero tolerance

Content involving minors, trafficking, non-consensual activity, credible violence or illegal exploitation is prohibited and escalated appropriately.

Prototype summary

Terms of Use

This concept demonstrates the expected structure of the production terms: eligibility and account rules, acceptable use, creator obligations, payment and refund terms, moderation powers, intellectual-property rules, disclaimers, termination and dispute provisions.

Before launch

Production terms must identify the operating business, supported jurisdictions, payment provider, recordkeeping process, complaint channel and governing law. The final version should be reviewed by qualified counsel for the jurisdictions served.

Privacy by design

Privacy Summary

The production notice should clearly explain account data, age/identity verification, creator documents, live and chat activity, payments, moderation records, cookies, retention, third-party processors, user rights and cross-border processing.

Sensitive data

Identity documents and verification results should be collected through a specialized provider, minimized, encrypted, access-limited and retained only as required.

User controls

Members need understandable tools to access, correct and delete eligible data, manage communications, block other users and submit privacy or safety concerns.
